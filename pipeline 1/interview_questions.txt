INTERVIEW QUESTIONS -- PIPELINE 1 (INGESTION: CHUNKING -> EMBEDDING -> VECTOR STORAGE)
=======================================================================================

CHUNKING
--------
1. Why chunk the document at all instead of feeding the whole book to the model?
2. Why did you choose paragraph-aware chunking instead of fixed-size (e.g., every
   500 characters) or whole-chapter chunking?
3. What's the trade-off of a smaller chunk size vs. a larger one?
4. Why measure chunk size in tokens instead of words? What bug did that catch for you?
5. What's a token, and why can one word become multiple tokens?
6. Why did you add overlap between chunks, and how did you pick the overlap size?
7. What happens if a single paragraph is longer than your max token limit? How would
   you handle that (your code currently assumes it never happens -- is that safe
   in general)?
8. How would your chunking approach need to change for a different kind of document
   (e.g., a legal contract, a codebase, a chat log)?

EMBEDDING
---------
9. What is an embedding, in plain terms?
10. Why did you pick multi-qa-MiniLM-L6-cos-v1 specifically, and what does
    "asymmetric query-vs-passage" mean?
11. Why does the model always output a fixed 384-number vector regardless of
    input length?
12. What does cosine similarity measure, and why does it matter that your chunking
    respects the model's token limit?
13. How do you know the embeddings actually capture meaning and not just keyword
    overlap? (You have a real example for this -- the "Dursleys" sentence pair.)
14. What would go wrong if you embedded queries and documents with two different
    models?
15. Why encode chunks in batches instead of one at a time?

VECTOR STORAGE
--------------
16. What is a vector database, and why not just store vectors in a regular SQL
    table or a Python list/file?
17. Explain the relationship between a client, a collection, and a record in
    ChromaDB.
18. Why did you explicitly configure hnsw:space: cosine? What happens if you don't?
19. What is HNSW, at a high level, and why doesn't it matter much at only 370
    vectors?
20. Why store the original chunk text and metadata alongside the vector instead of
    just the vector?
21. What's the difference between create_collection and get_or_create_collection,
    and why does that matter for re-running a script?
22. How would this design change if the book collection grew to millions of chunks
    instead of 370?

DEBUGGING / REAL INCIDENT (STRONG STORY FOR BEHAVIORAL QUESTIONS)
------------------------------------------------------------------
23. Tell me about a bug you found in this pipeline and how you diagnosed it. (The
    word-count vs. token-count chunk-size bug is a genuinely good STAR-format
    answer -- mention the model swap too.)
24. How did you verify your fix actually worked, rather than just assuming it did?

SYSTEM DESIGN / "WHY" QUESTIONS
--------------------------------
25. Walk me through what happens, step by step, from raw book text to a stored,
    searchable record.
26. What decisions in this pipeline were reversible vs. hard to change later, and
    how did that affect your choices?
27. If you had to add a second book to this system, what would need to change?
28. How would you know if your chunking/embedding choices were wrong -- what would
    you measure? (This nudges toward the evaluation pipeline, which hasn't been
    built yet.)
